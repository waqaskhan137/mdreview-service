## Error log

### E4 — I misread a coordinate-space mismatch as a broken feature
At 900px I real-clicked the dock's Comments button at its CSS-pixel centre (413,668). Nothing
happened, and `#gutter` stayed `display:none` with no `.docked` class. I was one step from filing
"the dock's Comments button does not open the panel on staging" as a #279 defect.
**It was my click that missed, not the button.** In a RESIZED window the extension's screenshot is
scaled (1232px wide for a 900px viewport, ratio 1.369) and `left_click` coordinates are in
SCREENSHOT space, not CSS space. My click at (413,668) landed at CSS (302,488) — empty article area.
Predicted the corrected target as (566,914), clicked (565,915), and the panel opened.
**Why I did not previously notice:** every earlier successful click this run was in a MAXIMISED
window, where the ratio is exactly 1 and the two spaces coincide.
**THE RULE, for every resized-window click from here on:** multiply CSS coordinates by
(screenshot width / window.innerWidth) before clicking, or read the target from a screenshot rather
than from getBoundingClientRect.
**What saved me:** before blaming #279 I checked whether it had touched the handler (it had not),
then probed a programmatic .click() across two poll cycles — it opened and PERSISTED, proving the
handler and the poll were both innocent and the fault was in my input.
**Falsified if:** the ratio is ever not (screenshot width / innerWidth) — it was exactly 1.369 here
and the prediction matched to 1px.
