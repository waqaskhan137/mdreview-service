v0.2.0 Latest Jul 24, 2026

Accounts, sharing, and a rebuilt interface

mdreview grows from a single-user tool into a hosted service you can share. Sign in with a magic link, keep your documents to yourself by default, then open one up — by public link or by inviting people you name. The whole interface was rebuilt on one design system along the way.

Accounts and per-document custody

Native sign-in, no third-party identity provider required. Every review belongs to whoever created it, and access is gated at a single custody choke point rather than per-route — so a document you did not create is not yours to read, comment on, or delete. Anonymous callers get nothing.

Sharing: public links and named people

Flip a review to a public, view-only link, or invite specific people by email with view or comment rights. Revocation takes effect immediately. Available on markdown and LaTeX reviews alike.

Admin console

Manage the people on your instance: grant or revoke admin, ban and unban, revoke tokens and live sessions, and keep an email/IP blocklist that is refused at the sign-in step.

Rebuilt UI on shared design tokens

One token layer drives every page, with light and dark following your system. The dashboard, account, and admin pages were rebuilt on component styles; the viewer gained a composer that sits beside the text it annotates, comments that stay anchored to their block as the document changes, and a version-history diff.

Agents connect with per-user API tokens minted from your account page and dropped into an MCP config once. The LaTeX review mode introduced in v0.1.0 now has full comment and sharing parity with markdown reviews.

v0.1.0 Jul 22, 2026

LaTeX paper review mode + template catalog

The review loop now handles LaTeX papers. Your agent creates a kind=latex review; you get an Overleaf-style split view — source on the left, a live-compiled PDF on the right — and comment on it exactly like a markdown draft.

Overleaf-style LaTeX review

Opt-in behind MDREVIEW_ENABLE_LATEX. A self-contained latex_review module compiles with Tectonic in a hardened, unprivileged container. Comments anchor to source lines; the PDF is always live, never stale. MCP gains a kind parameter.

Template catalog

create_review(kind=latex, template=<id>) seeds a paper from a bundled starter — IEEE, ACM, arXiv, Elsevier, LNCS. Non-CTAN conference styles (ACL, ICLR) download on first use from a pinned, sha256-verified registry and cache locally — never baked into the image.

Ships as a separate mdreview-service-latex image. The default slim image and flag-off behavior are byte-for-byte unchanged.
